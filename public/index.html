✓  What a T Level is

  • A Level 3 qualification equivalent to three A Levels.
  • Worth UCAS points — it can lead to university.
  • Built with employers, with a 45-day real placement.
  • A strong route into a skilled job or degree apprenticeship.

!  What it isn't (the honest part)

  • Not accepted by every single university course — some still ask for A Levels.
  • Not a light option: it's a serious, full-time commitment.
  • Not the right fit if your child wants a broad, non-technical mix of subjects.
  • Still newer than A Levels — so awareness varies (which is why this page exists).

"Is this just a backdoor because they couldn't get the grades?" +
No. T Levels are demanding Level 3 qualifications with entry requirements, and many students choose them over A Levels because they want the hands-on, career-focused route — not because it's easier. The stories on this site include students who turned down sixth-form A Level offers.
"Will my child be safe on a placement with a company?" +
Placements are with vetted employer partners and are supported and monitored by the college, with safeguarding agreements in place. Students are prepared for placement and have a college contact throughout.
"Can they still go to university afterwards?" +
Yes — a T Level carries UCAS points and graduates have progressed to universities including Queen Mary and Newcastle. We're transparent that not every course at every university accepts T Levels, so it's worth checking specific courses, and the team can help with that.
